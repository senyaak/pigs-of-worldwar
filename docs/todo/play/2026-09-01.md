# P0. WHAT TO CHECK IN PLAY — the 2026-09-01 batch, written for the session that tests it

Play is going to a fresh chat to test **TRENCH (mission 3)** and everything
built this day. This block is the handover: what changed, where it can be MET
in the campaign, and what a bug would look like against what is deliberate.
Each item names the section with the full story.

## 1. TRENCH's two tents give NOTHING, and that is correct (B20)

The first thing to expect is a negative. TRENCH carries two `TENT_S` and a
SHELTER, and **none of the three is a hospital** — `TENT_S` takes the shelter's
arm of the exe's own jump. A pig can sit in one all game and wake up no better.

**A bug would be the opposite**: if a pig heals in a `TENT_S`, our table is
wrong. The two that DO pay are `M_TENT1` (fifty points) and `M_TENT2` (full
health), and **the earliest one in the campaign is a MASH tent on SNIPER,
position 8** — five missions past TRENCH. Nothing before position 8 has one.

To try the heal at all before then: a shelter is on CAMP, RUMBLE and LIBERATE,
but none of them heals either. It needs SNIPER, GUNS, EMPLACE or BRIDGE.

## 2. The MORTAR — and it needs a BOMBARDIER, which no map fields (B18)

Skill 28 is in the BOMBARDIER's and the PYROTECH's kits only, so a squad has to
be PROMOTED into one (a gunner costs two points). What to watch:

- **no power gauge** — the press fires it at once, and the COME-UP is the whole
  of the aim. It cannot be pointed under 45°.
- one **fifty-point** burst on contact, then **three sub-shells** thrown almost
  straight up a third of a turn apart, twenty points each. 110 on one body at
  the worst.
- the camera should end up on the THIRD sub-shell, not the first.
- `[play]` to judge: the full-charge rule. The exe's own arithmetic would drop
  the shell on the pig's trotters, so the remake fires it at full — if the
  range feels wrong, that is the knob.

## 3. SUICIDE — ENGINEER, SABOTEUR, SNIPER (B18)

Fifty points over 2048 at the pig's own feet, and the pig dies. **It does not
take its own fifty** — it is removed first, which is the exe's order.

**One thing here is `[CHECK — remake]` and wants play's eye**: the exe takes the
pig out at the TOP of its arm, so its "Hari Kiri" clip 81 is never seen at all.
Ours plays the clip and lands the blast at its END. If that reads wrong, the
faithful version is instant.

## 4. THE GRENADE CAMERA — every map, every throw (B21)

The one to look at first, because it changes how everything thrown feels. The
lens no longer locks on the grenade: it chases it a third of the way a frame on
the flat and a SIXTH vertically, toward a point only a third of the way up from
the ground beneath it.

So a lob should **climb out of the middle of the frame** with the camera
panning up behind, and a CLUSTER's burst should **sweep** the aim across to the
bomblet rather than cutting to it. Measured 9° off the flight at the peak,
against 0.4° before.

## 5. THE BLAST'S THROW IS HALF WHAT IT WAS (B24)

Play measured it: "граната откидывает через чур далеко, раза в 3-4 дальше чем
надо." The throw stopped riding the damage and is the exe's own `force / mass`
now — a grenade launches at 86.7 a frame against 180, and because a throw is
ballistic that is about a QUARTER of the ground. On the bench a flat 45° toss
lands at 560 where it used to reach 1884.

So the thing to judge is whether it is now too SHORT. Everything else moved
with it: a mine throws harder than a grenade (it always did in the exe), TNT
harder again, and **the gases and the medicine ball throw nobody at all**,
which is a real zero in their own column rather than a special case.

## 6. SUPER TNT, if you want to see it (B25)

Skill 38 — which the repo used to call the FIRECRACKER and the game calls
**SUPER TNT** — is built: TNT's charge with **200 points** and the hardest
shove in the game. It comes out of a **CRATE**, on DESVAL, GUNS, SEPIA1 and
TWIN. Worth a look precisely because the throw just changed: it is the loudest
case there is.

**And the crate fix is the bigger thing to watch (B26)**: every crate byte
past 45 used to hand over NOTHING, which included the **JETPACK crate on
twenty-one maps** and both AIR STRIKES. Those now hand over a skill — and the
jetpack and the air strikes are still UNBUILT, so what you will meet is a
crate that gives you something the fire key ignores rather than a crate that
gives you nothing. That is the honest state, not a regression.

## 7. The AI with a mortar (B18)

A machine BOMBARDIER now solves its PITCH where every other lob solves the
gauge, and the turn's hand-shake moved onto the pitch with it. Two failure
shapes to watch for: shells landing on its own feet (the solve is wrong) or an
AI that never misses (the shake did not move).
